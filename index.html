Glossary & Data Notes

Data Source: Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics (BITRE) – Hospitalised Injury Data (2011–2021).

Note on Count Data: The dataset reports absolute counts of hospitalised injuries. Direct comparison of raw counts across jurisdictions (e.g., NSW or Victoria vs. Northern Territory) should take population size differences into account. Use the % Change Since 2011 mode and Average Bed Days views to compare relative growth and injury severity fairly across states.
